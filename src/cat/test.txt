Linus Torvalds and the creation of Linux

Linus Torvalds, a Finnish programmer, is known as the creator of the Linux operating system.
In 1991, as a student at the University of Helsinki, he released the first version of the Linux kernel.
Initially, it was just a hobby when he decided to create a free operating system for the x86 architecture,
inspired by the Minix system.







Key points			:
1. Year of creation: 1991
2. First version: Linux 0.01
3. The idea: To create a free and open operating system.
4. Impact: Linux has become the basis for many distributions and is used on servers, desktops and mobile devices.

Linux Development	:
Linux has grown significantly since its inception.
Today, it is used on more than 70% of all servers in the world and is the basis for most supercomputers.
Linus also developed the Git version control system in 2005, which simplified project management in an open source environment.

Linus's personality	:
Linus Torvalds is known not only for his technical achievements,
but also with his straightforward style of communication. In 2018, he announced the temporary cessation of his work on the Linux kernel in order
to work on his behavior in the developer community.

Quote				:
"I'm making a (free) operating system (just as a hobby,
it won't be big and professional...)" - Linus Torvalds about the beginning of Linux development.
